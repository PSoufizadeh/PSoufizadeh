## About

I am a veterinarian and data scientist working at the intersection of **clinical medicine, artificial intelligence and digital transformation**.

My research focuses on developing statistical and machine-learning models that translate routinely collected clinical data into interpretable tools for clinical prediction and decision-making.

Alongside my academic research, I serve as Head of AI & Digital in industry, focusing on applied AI, data-driven business planning and digital transformation.
